Mountaineers in Combat: The Nike Pro Combat Uniforms Revisted

June 7, 2021 By Ethan Dimitroff

In the days leading up to the 2010 football season Nike selected a few schools to be apart of Nike's Pro Combat uniform series. WVU was to be one of the schools to wear these special uniforms that had the latest uniform technology. The Mountaineers were scheduled to wear said uniforms at Heinz Field in Pittsburgh against the Pitt Panthers who also had Pro Combat uniforms of their own.

As far as the design of the uniforms they had very little of the traditional school colors of Gold and Blue but instead featured a coal design. The coal pattern adorned the helmets and various parts throughout the uniform. The uniforms were a tribute to the 29 coal miners who were killed in Upper Big Branch Mine disaster earlier in the year and had a memorial decal on the back of the helmets.
