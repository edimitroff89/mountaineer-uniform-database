Our story

Preserving Mountaineer history

The Mountaineer Uniform Database was created in 2016 by Ethan Dimitroff as a graphical archive of the uniforms worn by West Virginia University athletic programs.

Work on the project began several years earlier with extensive research, corrections, and the creation of detailed two-dimensional uniform artwork. What began as a project focused on West Virginia football soon expanded to include men’s basketball, women’s basketball, baseball, playing surfaces, and other parts of Mountaineer visual history.

The long-term goal is to build the most complete and accurate visual catalog possible for West Virginia’s major athletic programs.

How the database is researched

Research includes studying historic photographs, watching archived games, reviewing media guides, examining broadcast footage, attending games, and comparing details across multiple sources.

Older seasons can be especially challenging because clear photographs and video are not always available. Each design is updated when stronger visual evidence is discovered.

What the project covers

  • Football uniforms and season histories
  • Men’s basketball uniforms
  • Women’s basketball uniforms
  • Baseball uniforms
  • Mountaineer Field designs
  • Bowl-game field designs
  • WVU Coliseum floor designs
  • Uniform concepts and special designs

About the founder

Ethan Dimitroff

Ethan is the founder and artist behind the Mountaineer Uniform Database. He created the project to combine his interest in West Virginia athletics, uniform history, graphic design, and detailed sports research.

He has also contributed uniform tracking work to Uni Watch and has tracked uniform information across major college athletic conferences.
